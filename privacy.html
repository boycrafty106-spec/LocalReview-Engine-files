Privacy Policy

This MVP is designed to minimize data collection. Campaign details are stored in your browser using localStorage unless and until a future version introduces a disclosed server-side service.

Information you enter

Business setup information and campaign details entered into this MVP are stored locally in the browser. The product does not require customer accounts.

Customer review answers

Customer answers are processed in the browser by the MVP's local review-draft generator. Do not enter sensitive personal information.

External links

When a customer chooses to open a business's Google review URL, they leave LocalReview Engine and are subject to the destination service's policies.

Future changes

If a future version introduces accounts, analytics, AI APIs, payments, or server-side storage, this policy should be updated before those features launch.
