FAQ

Clear answers, no fine print games.

LocalReview Engine is designed around genuine customer feedback and transparent product behavior.

How does LocalReview Engine work?+

You create a campaign, generate a QR code, and share it with customers. Customers answer a few questions about their real experience, review an editable draft, and choose whether to continue to Google.

Do customers need an account?+

No. The MVP is designed for a frictionless customer experience without customer accounts.

Can customers edit their review?+

Yes. The generated draft is always editable before the customer copies it or opens Google.

Does this create fake reviews?+

No. The product must not fabricate experiences, ratings, claims, or testimonials. The review assistant only organizes information supplied by the customer.

Can I use my own Google review link?+

Yes. You provide the destination link during business setup. The MVP does not attempt to guess or impersonate your Google Business Profile.

Can I print the QR code?+

Yes. You can download the QR as an image or use the browser print flow.

Can I use it for multiple technicians?+

The data field exists in the MVP so a campaign can include a technician name. Dedicated technician campaign management is planned for a later version.

Is there a free plan?+

Yes. The free MVP includes one business and one campaign. Paid checkout is not yet implemented.
